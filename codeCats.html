Privacy Policy for CodeCats

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use the CodeCats mobile application ("the App", "the Service").

1. Information Collection and Use

CodeCats is an idle management game. It does not require an account, login, or any manually-entered personal information to play.

  • Game Progress: Your save data (coins, cats, upgrades, settings, etc.) is stored locally on your device only. We do not operate a server that stores your save file, and there is no cloud sync or account system.
  • Advertising Identifiers: The App shows optional, user-initiated rewarded video ads (you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward - ads never play automatically). These ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect device/advertising identifiers and other data as described in Google's own privacy policy (see Third-Party Services below).
  • Analytics: The App uses Firebase Analytics to understand how players interact with the game (for example: which desks get built, when cats are hired, tutorial completion, IPOs, and ad views), so we can improve the game. Firebase Analytics may also automatically collect standard usage/device data (such as app opens, session length, device model, and approximate location derived from IP address) as described in Google's Firebase privacy documentation.

We do not knowingly collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or any other directly-identifying personal information.

2. Permissions

To provide its core functionality, CodeCats may request the following permission:

  • Internet access: Required to show ads and send anonymous analytics events described above.

3. Data Retention

Your game progress is stored locally on your device for as long as the App is installed; deleting the App removes it. Data collected by AdMob and Firebase Analytics is retained and governed by Google's own retention policies (see their privacy policies linked below).

4. Third-Party Services

CodeCats uses the following third-party services, each of which may collect information used to identify you as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob - rewarded video ads
  • Google Firebase (Analytics) - anonymous gameplay analytics

We do not sell your information to third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

CodeCats is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
