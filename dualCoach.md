# Privacy Policy for DualCoach

**Last updated: April 04, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use the **DualCoach** mobile application.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

DualCoach is designed to prioritize your privacy. 

* **Personal Data:** We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) on our servers. 
* **User Profile:** Information such as your name, goals (Fitness, Productivity, etc.), and personality preferences (Bestie, Snarky, Beast) are stored **locally on your device** using secure internal storage.
* **Notifications:** All motivational messages are generated and scheduled locally on your device. We do not track the content of your notifications.

## 2. Permissions

To provide its core functionality, DualCoach may request the following permissions:
* **Notifications:** To send you motivational messages based on your selected profile.
* **Storage:** To save your preferences and app settings on your device.

## 3. Data Retention

Since your data is stored locally, it remains on your device as long as the app is installed. If you delete the app, all your personal settings and data will be permanently removed.

## 4. Disclaimer (Motivational Content)

DualCoach includes a "Beast Mode" (Drill Sergeant) and "Snarky Mode" that use aggressive, sarcastic, or dark humor for motivational purposes. 
* By using these modes, the user acknowledges that the messages are satirical and part of a roleplay experience.
* The app is not intended to provide professional psychological or medical advice.
* Users can switch to "Bestie Mode" (Positive) at any time.

## 5. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs that collect your data.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13.
